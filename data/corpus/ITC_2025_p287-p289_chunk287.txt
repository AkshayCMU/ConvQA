Terms and Conditions of Options Granted
Each Option entitles the holder thereof to apply for and be allotted ten Ordinary Shares of the Company of ` 1.00 each upon payment of the
exercise price during the exercise period. The exercise period commences from the date of vesting of the Options and expires at the end of five
years from the date of vesting in respect of Options.
The vesting period for conversion of Options is as follows:
On completion of 12 months from the date of grant of the Options : 30% vests
On completion of 24 months from the date of grant of the Options : 30% vests
On completion of 36 months from the date of grant of the Options : 40% vests
The Options have been granted at the ‘market price’ as defined under the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Share Based Employee
Benefits and Sweat Equity) Regulations, 2021.
Further details of ITC Employee Stock Option Schemes are provided in Note 30(xiv).
