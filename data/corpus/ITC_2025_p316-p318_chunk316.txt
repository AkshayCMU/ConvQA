16. Methodology for determination : The volatility used in the Black Scholes Option Pricing model is the annualised
of expected volatility standard deviation of the continuously compounded rates of return on the stock
over a period of time. The period considered for the working is commensurate with
the expected life of the Options and is based on the daily volatility of the Company’s
stock price on NSE. There are no market conditions attached to the grant and vest.
